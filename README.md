
# Retail Banking Operations: Digital Adoption & Queue Management Study

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an academic case study focused on retail banking operations, customer service optimization, and digital channel adoption. The primary objective is to analyze the causes of long waiting times (Turnaround Time / TAT) in bank branches and propose a structured framework to migrate routine transactions to digital platforms.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
* Analyze customer behavior and bottlenecks causing delays in traditional bank branches.
* Evaluate the challenges faced by customers (especially tech-hesitant individuals and senior citizens) in adopting mobile and digital banking.
* Propose a practical implementation framework—such as a **Digital Help Desk**—to improve branch efficiency and enhance customer satisfaction (CSAT).

---

## 🔍 Key Methodologies & Research
* **Sample Size:** Surveyed/interviewed 50+ retail bank customers to understand their primary reasons for visiting physical branches.
* **Customer Journey Mapping:** Mapped the end-to-end customer visit lifecycle from branch entry to teller interaction.
* **Problem Identification:** Found that over 40% of branch footfall was driven by routine, non-cash tasks (e.g., balance inquiries, mini-statements, UPI activation assistance).

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution & Framework
1. **Digital Help Desk Setup:** Establishing a dedicated desk in the branch lobby to guide walk-in customers instantly.
2. **Simplified Self-Service Guides:** Creating step-by-step pocket guides for mobile banking, net banking, and UPI setup.
3. **Counter Load Reduction:** Shifting low-value transactions to self-service channels, thereby reducing teller congestion and improving the overall **Turnaround Time (TAT) by an estimated 30-35%**.
4. **Cross-Selling Opportunity:** Reallocating freed-up staff time toward high-value advisory services, deposit mobilization (CASA), and product cross-selling (credit cards, insurance).

---

## 📈 Expected Impact & Outcomes
* Reduction in average customer waiting time (TAT) by **30-35%**.
* Increase in active mobile banking and UPI adoption rates among walk-in customers.
* Improved staff productivity, enabling better conversion rates on retail financial products.
